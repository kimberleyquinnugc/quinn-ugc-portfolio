Meet the Creator

Hi, I am Kimberley.

I am a UK-based UGC creator with a warm, clean aesthetic focused on beauty, skincare routines, and mindful everyday moments.

Whether I am showing how a cushion foundation blends seamlessly into skin, capturing the satisfying foam of a daily cleanser, or filming a cosy evening reset, my focus is always on sensory details, natural lighting, and honest demonstrations that build genuine trust.

I create both organic brand assets and performance-focused ad concepts tailored for TikTok and Meta feeds.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know.

What is your typical turnaround time?

Standard delivery is 5 to 7 business days from the date the product arrives at my studio in the UK. Express delivery options are available upon request.

Do you provide raw footage?

Yes. Raw B-roll packages and hook variation clips are available as add-ons or included in the Ad Testing and Campaign packages so your in-house team can test new iterations.

What usage rights are included?

All packages include organic social usage. Paid advertising licensing (30, 60, or 90 days) and spark ad whitelisting authorisation can be customised to your media plan.

What equipment and resolution do you deliver in?

All videos are delivered in high-definition 1080x1920 (9:16 vertical), shot with native sound recording, studio lighting, and safe-zone compliance for TikTok and Instagram reels.
